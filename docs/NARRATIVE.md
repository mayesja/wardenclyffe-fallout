# Wardenclyffe Field Station: New York State Expansion Blueprint

This document outlines the structural, mechanical, and narrative overhaul for **Wardenclyffe Field Station**, pivoting the game into a forward-only, multi-branching incremental progression across New York State[cite: 7].

---

## 1. THE GEOGRAPHIC CORE & THEMATIC PROGRESSION

The gameplay follows a strict forward-only model inspired by *Universal Paperclips*[cite: 7]. Players cannot return to past locations; instead, completing a region triggers a **Migration Sequence** where the UI panel completely resets, transferring only the scaled essence of your achievements into an expanded operational paradigm[cite: 7].

*   **Stage 0: Wardenclyffe (The Isolated Awakening):** Setting the baseline tutorial[cite: 7]. Focuses on manual kinetic friction, dead silence, and awakening an abandoned coastal bunker in the wake of *The Great Collapse*[cite: 7].
*   **Stage 1: Poughkeepsie (The Kinetic Generation):** Harnessing the heavy currents of the Hudson River to transition into automated, industrial power generation[cite: 7].
*   **Stage 2: Ithaca (The Gorge Fortress & Betrayal):** Shifting focus to long-distance transmission, data protection, and localized grid survival inside rocky gorges[cite: 7]. Features a major plot twist where an ally betrays the player[cite: 7].
*   **Stage 3: Niagara Falls (The Macro-Harnessing Endgame):** Scaling wires and Joules exponentially by tapping the infinite kinetic force of the falls with colossal resonant monoliths to permanently shatter the Edison Trust[cite: 1, 7].
*   **Stage 4: Global Resonance (The Climax):** Firing the unified state grid to trigger one of **8 distinct narrative endings** based on cumulative player choices[cite: 7].

---

## 2. THE DEFINITIVE REGIONAL GAMEPLAY LOOP

Every automated outpost operates on a standardized, escalating lifecycle[cite: 7]:

1.  **Arrive & Reset:** Boot up a fresh interface, unpack the previous stage's success, and anchor to the local geographic resource[cite: 7]. Numbers and capacities jump significantly in scale[cite: 7].
2.  **Build Base Infrastructure:** Accumulate a baseline pool of Joules and Wire to establish a local presence[cite: 7].
3.  **Intercept & Decrypt:** Hit a resource threshold to trigger a Morse decryption clock, intercepting a cryptic transmission from the underground network[cite: 7].
4.  **Choose the Branch (The Fork):** Lock in an ideological choice, permanently altering active metrics, UI labels, and unlocking unique technology trees[cite: 7].
5.  **Survive the Escalation (Threat Phase):** The choice alerts the Edison Trust[cite: 7]. Active automated raid loops begin draining resources and locking out generation until defenses are built[cite: 7].
6.  **Construct the Regional Monolith:** Scramble to save up and construct high-tier architectural infrastructure[cite: 7].
7.  **Secure the Zone & Reset:** Completing the monolith triggers a massive energy surge that permanently neutralizes local Trust enforcers and halts automated attacks[cite: 7].
8.  **Migrate Forward:** Transfer data packages, trigger the migration UI reset, and abandon the facility for the next city[cite: 7].

## 3. THE REASSESSED TECHNOLOGY TREE & THE REAL-WORLD COILS

### Stage 0: Primitive Survival Tech
To ground the game in a physical engineering reality, Stage 0 focuses entirely on mechanical reclamation:
*   `[ CRANK DYNAMO ]`: Manual, exhausting input (+1 Joule per click)[cite: 7].
*   `[ FORGE COPPER WIRING ]`: The foundational material sink[cite: 7].
*   `[ BUILD PNEUMATIC LOOM ]`: Relieves the player of manual forging, turning Joules into automatic Wiring[cite: 7].
*   `[ RECLAIM BLUFF-SIDE TURBINE ]`: *Replaces early-game Tesla Coils.* A crude mechanical generator on the coastal cliffs providing basic passive generation (+1J/sec)[cite: 7].

### The Evolution of the Tesla Coil
Tesla Coils are saved for later stages, transforming them from boring generators into high-tier, specialized narrative payoffs:
1.  **Stage 1 (Poughkeepsie) — The Defensive Spark-Gap Coil:** Uses a fraction of active generation to create an automated perimeter defense grid, instantly vaporizing Trust wire-cutting raids[cite: 7].
2.  **Stage 2 (Ithaca) — The Transmission Relay Mast:** A medium-sized tower allowing wireless power transmission over short distances, letting the player power hidden pocket grids in the mountains without laying traceable physical copper lines[cite: 7].
3.  **Stage 3 (Niagara Falls) — The Grand Wardenclyffe Monolith:** A colossal, hyper-resonant broadcasting tower that broadcasts free, un-metered wireless energy across the entire sky to win the game[cite: 7].

## 4. MULTI-BRANCHING STORY CRITICALITY

### Fork 1: The Wardenclyffe Decision (AC vs. DC Baseline)
*   **The AC Choice:** Long-range atmospheric resonance, high-voltage induction, step-up transformers, and high-risk/high-yield automation[cite: 7].
*   **The DC Choice:** Constant-current density, heavy localized battery containment (Leyden Jars), thick insulated wiring, and defensive fortifying[cite: 7].

### Fork 2: The Poughkeepsie Decision (Distribution Philosophy)
Once the player scales their river grid, they face a choice between **Centralization** vs. **Distribution**:
*   **Timeline AC Splits:** 
    *   *Choice AC-1 (Centralized Induction Tower):* Maximizes raw power yield (`+5J/s` AC Generators) but creates a massive electromagnetic signature, resulting in fast, aggressive Trust raids[cite: 7].
    *   *Choice AC-2 (Distributed River Mesh):* Splinter current into low-profile, synchronized hydro-buoys chained across the Hudson to lower corporate exposure[cite: 7].
*   **Timeline DC Splits:**
    *   *Choice DC-1 (Subterranean Vault Bank):* Anchors the grid into bedrock cliffs, maximizing storage caps and armor plating against resource theft[cite: 7].
    *   *Choice DC-2 (Municipal Grid Parasite):* Quietly bleeds current into local town streetlamp infrastructure, tying passive generation to stealthy grid corruption[cite: 1, 7].

### Fork 3: The Ithaca Decision & The Narrative Twist
Deep in Ithaca, the underground sister outpost betrays the player to buy corporate immunity from the Edison Trust[cite: 7]. A massive, overwhelming corporate raid ensues. Just before total failure, a rogue faction *inside* the Edison Trust rebels against their coal-burning directors, overriding the terminal to unlock the northern transit grid and guide the player to Niagara[cite: 7].
